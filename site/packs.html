Modpacks and config

The settings, the commands, the tags a pack can change, and how The End That Was Promised sits beside other mods.

Config

NeoForge: config/promisedend-synced.toml, sent from the server to every player. Fabric: config/promisedend.properties, written with the defaults on first start and read at startup. The settings are the same on both.

Commands

For operators (permission level 2):

  • /promisedend wrath get, /promisedend wrath set <0-5>: the world's Dragon Wrath, held under max_wrath.
  • /promisedend seals get, /promisedend seals break <1-3>, /promisedend seals reset: the Ancients' Ward's seals (break one as its guardian's fall does).
  • /promisedend starfall now: a star falls near you now.
  • /promisedend codex reset <players>: forgets every Codex goal (the tablets' pages stay read).

The ruins are found with vanilla's /locate structure.

Every new biome is in #minecraft:is_end and #c:is_end, so other mods' End spawns that use those tags reach them.

Other mods

  • Another End biome mod: set terrain.enabled to false. The new biomes then stay out, and the rest still works: the Rim's islands and outposts and the guardians' ruins come to the End biomes that are there instead.
  • The dragon: its third phase and Wrath can each be turned off (dragon.phase3, dragon.wrath).
  • The arrival: with arrival.by_coordinates off, portals lead to vanilla's platform and the dragon at once, and there is no Ward.
  • The new gear is made of plain tool and armour items, and the elytra augments leave the elytra a vanilla elytra.
